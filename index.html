Franchise Opportunity Profile

Anytime Fitness

Anytime Fitness is a 24-hour fitness center franchise founded in 2002 in Hastings, Minnesota, now headquartered in Woodbury, Minnesota. Every fact on this page is sourced from the Anytime Fitness Franchisor LLC 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document (issued March 31, 2026), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$42,500

Standard, non-refundable, for one center. Veterans $38,250; existing franchisees $37,500; Club Purple $27,500; Club Platinum $22,500. Area development fees: $75,000 (2), $97,500 (3), $130,000 (4). (FDD Item 5, pp.8-9)

Estimated Initial Investment

$539,329 to $905,482

$37,857-$45,462 payable to affiliate ProVision (technology system package); $11,000-$23,000 grand opening spend. No franchisor financing. (FDD Item 5, p.9; Item 7, p.19)

Monthly Fee (Royalty)

$842/mo Monthly Fee (may convert to up to 8% of Gross Revenue)

Fixed monthly fee, subject to annual CPI increase; includes AF Coaching fee. May be replaced with up to 8% of Gross Revenue. (FDD Item 6, pp.11-12)

Advertising

$900/mo General Advertising Fund

General Advertising Fund; may increase on 60 days notice (capped at greater of $900/mo or 3% of Gross Revenue). Local marketing $600/$800/$1,000/month by market tier. (FDD Item 6, pp.11-15)

Technology Fee

$799/month

Base Technology Fee, subject to annual 10% compounded increase. (FDD Item 6, p.12)

Territory

Protected, not exclusive

Circle up to 3-mile radius, 30,000 population; protected territories may overlap; competition from other franchisees or franchisor channels is possible. (FDD Item 12, pp.44-45)

Term

6 years + 5-year renewal

Renewal requires new agreement, then-current standards, release, $7,500 renewal fee. Post-term non-compete 2 years / 10 miles (5 miles in metros >50,000). (FDD Item 17, pp.51-53)

Facility

4,000 to 7,000 sq ft

Vanilla-shell/as-is space; 2025 lease avg $25.13/sq ft combined ($19.08 base rent + $6.05 CAM) with landlord TI allowance avg $27.23/sq ft; 3 months rent/security deposit $33,500-$58,700. (FDD Item 7, p.19)

Item 19 Revenue

$446,814 avg

Average total revenue across 1,683 franchised AF Coaching centers (12 months ended 2026-02-28); median $398,982; high $2,048,737; low $90,337. (FDD Item 19, p.55)

Owner Model

Semi-absentee OK

Owner need not personally supervise, but a trained Principal Operator is required; each owner (and spouse) signs a personal guaranty. (FDD Item 15, p.49)

HQ

Woodbury, Minnesota

Anytime Fitness Franchisor LLC, 111 Weir Drive, Woodbury, MN 55125. Delaware LLC formed 2021-10-25; brand franchised since 2002. (FDD Item 1, p.1)

System Size

2,282 outlets

2,271 franchised + 11 company-owned as of 12/31/2025; net -19 in 2025; -47 over three years. (FDD Item 20, pp.60-61)

Risk Profile & Disclosure

42 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Moderate-Low. A mature system (2,282 outlets) with broad Item 19 reporting covering 74% of franchised centers, tempered by high capital intensity, a heavy fixed-fee load of about $2,541 per month before local marketing, three Spain affiliate litigation matters with two pending, a non-exclusive territory, and three straight years of net unit contraction.

Risk inputs (documented):

  • Capital intensity (+10): Investment range tops $905,482 with a $42,500 nonrefundable initial franchise fee and no franchisor financing. (FDD Item 7, p.19)
  • Fixed fee load (+6): $842/mo Monthly Fee + $900/mo advertising + $799/mo technology = $2,541/mo before local marketing spend. (FDD Item 6, pp.11-12)
  • Litigation exposure (+6): Three Spain (Anytime Fitness Iberia) franchisee disputes — two pending, one dismissed and on appeal — plus two settled Bar Method affiliate regulatory matters. (FDD Item 3, pp.7-8)
  • Non-exclusive territory (+4): Protected circle only — up to 3 miles, 30,000 population; protected territories may overlap and competition from other franchisees or franchisor channels is permitted. (FDD Item 12, pp.44-45)
  • Outlet closures (+4): 39 terminations and 3 other closures in 2025 — about 1.8% of the franchised base — plus 30 non-renewals. (FDD Item 20, Table 3, p.66)
  • System contraction (+3): Franchised count fell by 47 net outlets from 2023 to 2025, from 2,318 to 2,271. (FDD Item 20, Table 1, pp.60-61)
  • Renewal friction (+2): Renewal requires a new (possibly materially different) agreement, then-current standards, a general release, and a $7,500 fee. (FDD Item 17, p.51)
  • Item 19 strength (-6): Item 19 covers 1,683 franchised centers — 74% of the system — average total revenue $446,814; company-owned EBITDA $149,620 (28.67% margin). (FDD Item 19, pp.54-59)
  • Audited financials (-2): Audited financial statements of affiliate SEB Franchising Guarantor LLC attached at Exhibit D. (FDD Item 21, p.69)
